\# Deckoard



> 3D Board Graphics Studio \& Custom Deck Designer



Design Custom Graphics for Your Board in Real-Time 3D.



Website: https://www.deckoard.com/



Deckoard is a lightweight, web-first 3D board graphics studio designed for skateboarders, digital artists, and custom deck creators. It eliminates the guesswork of flat 2D templates by rendering your artwork onto real, interactive 3D board models with authentic curves, concaves, kicks, and contours.



Key Highlights:

&#x20;- Interactive 3D Preview: Rotate and inspect your graphics in real time from any angle to see exactly how they look wrapped over actual deck geometry.

&#x20;- Dual-Surface Editing: Independently design both the bottom graphic and top grip tape surface with full layer controls, text, shapes, and brush tools.

&#x20;- Zero Friction \& Local Privacy: Completely free to start with no account or registration needed. All uploaded artwork and images are processed securely on your local device.

&#x20;- Production-Ready Exports: Instantly download high-resolution 300 DPI PNG files with standard 0.25-inch bleed, ready for physical deck manufacturing, screen printing, or vinyl wrapping.

&#x20;- Vendor Agnostic: You own your artwork. Deckoard gives you standard high-res production files to take to any local print shop or board manufacturer of your choice.
